Signal Guide

Understanding the intelligence indicators presented across the platform. These are interpretation guides, not conclusions.

ANALYTICAL NOTICE

Signals are interpretation guides, not conclusions. Avoid drawing strong judgments from isolated data points. Always seek corroboration across multiple data layers before forming assessments.

CRITICAL

Confirmed high-impact activity requiring immediate attention. Verified by 3+ independent sources with high confidence.

HIGH

Strong indicators of significant activity. Corroborated by at least 2 independent sources. Warrants elevated monitoring.

MODERATE

Notable patterns detected from single-source reporting. Requires additional corroboration before actionable assessment.

LOW

Ambient noise or routine activity. Monitored for trend analysis. May become significant when aggregated over time.

INFO

Contextual data for situational awareness. No immediate action required. Background reference material for analysts.

Core Principles

  • 01 No single signal constitutes actionable intelligence in isolation
  • 02 Confidence increases with independent source corroboration
  • 03 Context determines significance — identical signals carry different weight in different environments
  • 04 Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence
  • 05 All assessments carry inherent uncertainty — communicate confidence levels explicitly
